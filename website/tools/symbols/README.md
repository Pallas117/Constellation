# Refined brand symbols (source copies)

Flat vector symbols from the Lightbound Design System artifact ("Symbols" group, refined 16–18 Oct 2026),
https://claude.ai/artifact/CvWgPQyJio2m5uHPyZZHdv. Copied unchanged; the Lightbound Brand Book (15 Oct, with
16 and 18 Oct updates) is the rule set:

- Lightbound "Black hole": the ray-traced silhouette, flat, 3-stop gradient, ring 7 units (no blur).
- Gauss Aurora "Radiation belts": two solid belts (outer lime, inner amber), one L 6.4 field line; reads at 16 px.
- Turnstone v9, 5 Nov (Josh: "more realistic and less chunky", "cute but deadly", "look again at images of the
  turnstone bird"; proposed), drawn from photos of the ruddy turnstone: long pointed wings raised in a V with the dark
  leading edge in earth blue; the harlequin mask through the eye, its cheek stripe dropping into the black breast band;
  a catch-light in the masked eye; plump white belly, short orange legs, the two-step tide line. Published as -v3.
  Minimum 32 px; below that use the badge.
- Constellation, refined 4 Nov: navy disc, gold ring broken by five evenly spaced stars, three gold key stars on an
  equilateral triangle with one white star off each. The 3-node icon is for 32 px and under.
- Argo, 5 Nov (Josh: "Argo could be just a sail", then "a sail in the wind"; both approved): a square sail seen from the
  side, its seams sagging down-wind and its free edge bellied out; fine mast, yard and sheet, three panels with fine gaps, set by Canopus (the
  lime four-point star). On a black tile it is the favicon.
- Gauss Aurora icon, 5 Nov: the symbol's own belts cropped square with a solid warm Earth, for favicons and app icons
  (the full mark is 2.45:1 and loses its field line in a square).

The Constellation, Turnstone and Argo files and the Gauss icon are generated by `website/tools/make_marks.py` (run it, not a hand edit);
copy them back to the Design System so it stays the source for decks. Published marks are cached for a week, so
a changed mark gets a new published name (`-v2`).

`website/tools/make_lockups.py` builds the horizontal lockups from these (wordmarks kerned with HarfBuzz since 5 Nov); `build_assets.py` copies them into
`public/assets/marks/`. Do not hand-edit the symbols here: Lightbound and Gauss come from the Design System; Constellation and Turnstone
from make_marks.py.
