Ethical use

Our principles follow the space community’s own: space for peaceful purposes, as in the Outer Space Treaty and the Artemis Accords; open, shared science, as in ESA’s and NASA’s data policies; and NASA’s principles for ethical AI (fair, explainable, accountable, secure and safe, human-centred, and scientifically robust). In practice, you agree to:

  • Use it for peaceful purposes: discovery, research, education, safe operations and resilience. Not to plan or carry out attacks, to target people, or to develop weapons.
  • Do no harm: no unlawful surveillance of people, no harassment, and no interfering with other people’s systems, networks or space operations.
  • Keep a person in charge: Gauss is advisory. It shows evidence and never commands a spacecraft; decisions stay with qualified people.
  • Be honest about what it is: present Gauss results as evidence and advice, never as an approval to fly, and keep synthetic examples labelled as synthetic.
  • Keep it secure: keep your sign-in to yourself, and tell us about security problems you find (jzwnathan@lightbound.uk) rather than exploiting them.
  • Follow the law: including export-control and sanctions rules. Our services aren’t for people or places those rules exclude.

In return, we explain how Gauss works and where it stops, label what is synthetic or planned, keep only the data we need (see the privacy notice), and treat your data as yours: we use it only to provide the service.

  • Constellation membership, events and the student plan of Gauss for Education are free.

None of this reduces your rights under consumer law.
