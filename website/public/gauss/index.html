01_GAUSS · Ground-first mission-data assurance

Know which satellite data and onboard-AI outputs you can trust.

Radiation and space weather can silently corrupt telemetry and AI results in orbit. A corrupted output looks the same as a good one. Gauss replays your telemetry and inference logs against the space environment they were produced in, and shows your ground team what held up, what didn't, and what is unknown.

Built for onboard-AI and orbital-compute teams, and satellite operations teams who need evidence, not dashboards.

02_HOW_IT_WORKS

How a replay pilot works

Fleet Integration v0.1 runs on the ground, on your own logs. Nothing is installed on a spacecraft.

  1. Bring your logs

    Telemetry (CCSDS/XTCE-shaped packets or Yamcs exports) and onboard inference logs, shared under a data-sharing agreement.

  2. Correlate with the space environment

    Each record is lined up with the space-weather conditions at the time: radiation and geomagnetic activity from public NOAA and NASA HAPI services.

  3. Score quality and provenance

    Every value carries its source and age. Bounded inference with a deterministic fallback flags outputs taken under stress. Missing or stale inputs stay unknown, never "nominal".

  4. Get the evidence

    A report your engineers and assurance reviewers can check, with success metrics agreed before the pilot starts.

Status: a working software prototype ingests live NOAA and NASA HAPI space-weather data today. Ingest of your own logs and the evidence export are being built for the first pilots.

04_CLAIMS

What we claim, and what we don't

We will show you

  • Which records were produced under elevated radiation or geomagnetic stress
  • Where data quality or provenance is missing, with "unknown" said plainly
  • Simulation results labelled as simulation

We don't claim

  • Flight readiness or space qualification
  • Radiation dose or hardware damage figures
  • Any ability to command a spacecraft: Gauss is advisory only
