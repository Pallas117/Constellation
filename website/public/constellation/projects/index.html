    02_HOW_IT_WORKS

    How a listing goes up.

    1. Tell us

      A project you or your team made, or the channel, podcast or newsletter where you cover space. Not a member yet? Ask to join in the same form; membership is free.

    2. A person reads it

      We list work that’s real, made by people in the community or made for it, and honest about what it is.

    3. You approve the wording

      We email you the exact listing. Nothing goes up until you say yes.

    4. Share it

      Every listing has its own link to share. Ask and it comes down, no questions.

    Listing is free, and no one can buy a place or a higher spot.
