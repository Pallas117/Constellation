04_INTEL_RULES

How we share.

  • Open sources only. Every brief links to where it came from.
  • Unclassified only. Nothing export-controlled, nothing operational.
  • Confidence is stated: confirmed, likely or unverified.
  • No targeting, and no personal data about individuals.

06_WORKSHOPS

Ethics, resilience and tech leadership.

Hands-on workshops for people who build and run space and AI systems, led by facilitators from the Constellation community. Members join free; dates go on the Luma calendar.

  1. Ethics

    Dual use, data and the choices engineers make, worked through on real cases from space and AI.

  2. Resilience

    How systems and the people who run them recover: fault isolation from spacecraft, applied to teams and careers.

  3. Tech leadership

    Deciding under uncertainty, leading with evidence, and explaining risk to people who aren’t engineers.
