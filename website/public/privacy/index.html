PRIVACY

What we keep, and why.

Lightbound Limited (company no. 14573199, 30A Commercial Street, Harrogate, HG1 1AX) runs this site and decides how this data is used.

Applications

When you apply to join Constellation or for a Gauss plan, or join the Argo list, we keep what you type into the form (name, email, organisation or role, an optional link, your message, and for Argo your platform and number of devices) so a person can review it. Applications are deleted automatically 180 days after they arrive.

Accounts

If we approve you, we keep your email address, name, organisation, your Gauss plan or Constellation membership, and where your pilot is up to, for as long as you have an account.

Signing in

Sign-in to the Gauss workspace is handled by Cloudflare Access: it emails you a one-time code and sets a sign-in cookie for the workspace only. We don't use passwords, advertising or analytics cookies, or trackers.

Payments

Gauss plans are paid through Stripe, on Stripe's own pages. Stripe tells us whether a payment succeeded and gives us a reference for your billing account; your card or bank details never reach Lightbound. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires.

Who processes it

The site and its data run on Cloudflare. Payments run on Stripe. Launch and space-weather data come from public sources (The Space Devs, NOAA) and contain nothing about you.

Your rights

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email jzwnathan@lightbound.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
