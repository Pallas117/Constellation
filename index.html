The Overview Summit The Cognitive Shift
Theoretical Paradigm

The Overview Effect

First coined by space philosopher Frank White in his seminal 1987 work, the Overview Effect describes the profound cognitive shift experienced by astronauts viewing the Earth from orbit.

From deep space, national boundaries vanish, conflicts seem trivial, and the absolute necessity of establishing unified global structures to safeguard our fragile planetary capsule becomes instantly obvious. We operationalize this perspective on Earth.

Inspirational Focal

Frank White

"To explore space is to change our relationship to the Earth and to each other."
